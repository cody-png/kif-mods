# Megas

Mega Evolution and Primal Reversion for KIF (Kuray's Infinite Fusion), fusions included. This page describes Megas 0.5.7.

## What it adds

- Mega Evolution and Primal Reversion, fusions included.
- 70 official Megas, plus Primal Kyogre and Primal Groudon. (Mega Rayquaza is one of the 70 but needs KIF: Elite Redux; see below.)
- With the KIF: Elite Redux mod: 61 more Elite Redux forms, 60 Megas (for example Mega Charizard Z) plus Primal Snorlax.
- After Silph Co, Gym Leaders, the Elite Four, Champion Blue, Rocket Boss Giovanni and your rival Blue Mega Evolve too.
- The Z-A abilities: Mega Sol (Mega Meganium), Aura Guard (Mega Lucario Z), Sharpness (Mega Absol Z), Unseen Fist (Mega Golurk), Eelevate (Mega Eelektross), Stalwart (Mega Skarmory). With KIF: Elite Redux's Innates On, these Megas use their Elite Redux abilities instead; Mega Meganium keeps Mega Sol (Elite Redux's version).
- Sprites for every Mega. Pokédex entries for the official Megas and Primals, on the Pokédex page you open from the Summary (Elite Redux Megas and Mega fusions keep the normal page).

## Installing

Options > Mods > Browse > KIF Mods (cody-png) > Megas. It starts working the next time the game starts. If Mods is greyed out, set Options > KIF Settings > Modules > Mod Settings to On first.

## Getting Mega Stones

- **Where:** Saffron City's Poké Mart, from the **right-hand** of the two clerks at the counter (the one who also sells the Psychic Gem, Fighting Gem and Friend Ball). The left-hand clerk only has the usual stock. No other mart sells stones, and nothing else in the game gives them.
- **When:** right after you beat Giovanni on Silph Co 11F. Until then, the Saffron mart door leads to Team Rocket's Rocket Mart, which has no stones.
- **Batches** (by the Pokémon's dex number, the same in Classic and Remix):
  - After Silph Co, no badges needed: Pokémon #1-151 except Mewtwo, e.g. Charizardite X/Y, Gengarite, Gyaradosite.
  - 7 badges: #152-251, e.g. Tyranitarite, Scizorite, Ampharosite.
  - 8 badges: every other Pokémon, including later evolutions of older families such as Magnezone and Weavile.
  - After you beat Champion Blue (Hall of Fame): Mewtwonite X and Y, Latiasite, Latiosite, Diancite, the Blue Orb and the Red Orb.
  - Any badges count; only how many you have matters.
- **How many:** without KIF: Elite Redux, 71 stones and orbs (19 after Silph Co, 27 at 7 badges, 64 at 8 badges, 71 after the Champion). With KIF: Elite Redux and its Innates setting On, 132 (43, 63, 125, 132).
- **Price:** 10,000 each; they sell back for 5,000.
- Stones are normal items in the Items pocket, not key items, so you can buy two of the same stone.
- KIF's Random Shop Items setting never replaces a Mega Stone or orb.

## How Mega Evolution works

- **No Mega Ring needed.** The Pokémon only has to hold its own stone. The battle message reads "Charizard's Charizardite X is reacting!".
- **Settings:** Options > KIF Settings > Mods > Megas settings. If Mods is greyed out, set Options > KIF Settings > Modules > Mod Settings to On. The settings apply to every save file.
- **Mega mode** (a change takes effect at once):
  - **Once per battle** (default): in the Fight menu press the Action key (Z, or Shift) to turn Mega Evolution on, then pick a move. One Mega Evolution per trainer per battle, wild battles included. In a double battle you still get one, and your partner has their own. Your Pokémon turn back after the battle, or when they faint.
  - **Permanent:** the first stone holder in your party is Mega all the time, in and out of battle. It keeps that slot until it faints, goes to the PC or loses its stone. Your other stone holders can't Mega Evolve.
  - **Permanent+:** every stone holder in your party is Mega all the time, so a team of six Megas works.
  - In both Permanent modes, Megas have no Mega button. After giving or taking a stone, or putting a Mega in the PC, open your party's Summary or start a battle to update who is Mega.
- **Mega fusion stats:** Full (default) or Split; see Fusions.
- **Primals:** Kyogre with the Blue Orb, Groudon with the Red Orb, and with KIF: Elite Redux, Snorlax with Primal Snorlaxite.
  - Primal trigger **Auto** (default): it reverts the moment it is sent out.
  - Primal trigger **Manual**: use the Mega button (also in the Permanent modes).
  - A Primal never uses up your Mega Evolution, before or after a Mega.
  - In both Permanent modes, Primals stay Primal after battle and don't take the Permanent slot.
- A stone can't be knocked off, stolen or swapped away from a Pokémon that can use it.
- No Mega Evolution while the Pokémon is held by Sky Drop, or while Magic Room is up (KIF: Elite Redux, Moves setting On).
- Mega Evolving doesn't change a Pokémon's HP (except a self-fusion with KIF's Self-Fusion Stat Boost On; see Fusions).
- Wild Pokémon never Mega Evolve. Fainted Pokémon are never Mega.
- **Mega Rayquaza** needs no stone, only the move Dragon Ascent. With KIF: Elite Redux's Innates and Moves settings On (both default), Rayquaza learns it at level 56, so the level 70 Rayquaza at Sky Pillar already knows it. Without KIF: Elite Redux, Rayquaza can't learn Dragon Ascent.

## Fusions

- **One stone:** a fusion holding a stone Mega Evolves the half that stone fits. Without KIF: Elite Redux, the fusion gets that Mega half's ability. With it on, the fusion keeps its Elite Redux ability pick, where the Mega half's choices replace that half's own.
- **Same species twice** (Charizard × Charizard) with one stone: one half goes Mega. When you fuse two Pokémon of a species that has a Mega Stone, the game asks "Head" or "Body" right after; cancelling picks Head. If you fused them before they evolved (for example two Charmander), Head is used. To change it, unfuse and fuse again.
- **Both halves Mega:** the fusion holds one Mega Stone and the other half's stone sits in your bag.
  - It works with two copies of the same stone or two different stones, and either stone can be the held one.
  - Example: Charizard × Blastoise holding Charizardite X, with Blastoisinite in the bag, is Mega Charizard X and Mega Blastoise at once.
  - The bag stone isn't used up, and it counts for every fusion in your party.
  - If the bag has a spare copy of the held stone, that copy is used first. Holding Charizardite Y with both X and Y in the bag gives Y × Y; keep only Charizardite X in the bag to get Y × X.
  - Only Mega Stones count from the bag, not orbs. A fusion can't be Mega and Primal at once.
  - Stones in the bag with nothing held do nothing (except a Rayquaza fusion that knows Dragon Ascent).
  - Both halves Mega: the head Mega's ability if the fusion's ability slot is the head's, otherwise the body Mega's. With KIF: Elite Redux on, its Elite Redux pick.
- **Stats:** the Megas setting **Mega fusion stats** decides how much a Mega half adds. HP never changes.
  - **Full** (default): each Mega half adds its whole Mega boost to the fusion, +100 BST for an official Mega. Charizard × Charizard goes from 531 to 631 with one half Mega and to 731 as Mega X × Mega X. Charizard × Blastoise goes from 529 to 729 with both halves Mega.
  - **Split:** the boost is shared like any fusion stat, 2/3 from one half and 1/3 from the other (the head leads HP, Sp. Atk and Sp. Def; the body leads Attack, Defense and Speed). One half Mega adds about +40 to +60 and a double Mega about +100: Charizard × Charizard as Mega X × Mega X is 630.
  - With KIF: Elite Redux on, each Mega's boost is its Elite Redux one, so the numbers shift a little per Pokémon.
- **Fusing a Mega** works as normal. It turns back first, and both held items go to your bag, so give the stone back to the fusion afterwards.
- Triple fusions can't Mega Evolve.
- KIF's Self-Fusion Stat Boost setting (Off by default) keeps working for a self-fusion with no Mega. For a double Mega of the same Mega (X × X) it still applies, but the higher stat total gives a smaller boost, so HP drops a little. With one half Mega, or X × Y, the boost is off, so Mega Evolving can lower the stats, HP included.

## Trainers

- **From when:** right after you beat Giovanni on Silph Co 11F. The Silph Co fights themselves have no Megas.
- **Who:**
  - Every Kanto Gym Leader, and the Johto leaders (Falkner, Kurt, Whitney, Morty, Chuck, Jasmine, Pryce, Clair).
  - The Elite Four, Champion Blue, Rocket Boss Giovanni and your rival Blue, rematches included.
  - Not Cynthia, and never ordinary trainers.
- **One Mega each:** the trainer's ace gets a stone at the start of the battle.
  - The ace is the highest-level Pokémon on the team that can Mega Evolve, not always their strongest Pokémon.
  - The stone replaces what the ace was holding, for example Sabrina's Twisted Spoon.
  - A fused ace Mega Evolves one half. If both halves could, the half is picked at random each fight. Trainers never double-Mega.
  - A team that already holds a Mega Stone in its data is left alone. Randomized teams follow the same rule.
  - A trainer whose team has nothing that can Mega Evolve has no Mega. Without KIF: Elite Redux this includes Pryce in Classic, Giovanni's first Viridian Gym fight in Classic, your rival Blue until his level 75 fight in Remix, and some Rocket Boss Giovanni fights.
- **Mega mode:** trainers follow the same setting as you.
  - In Once per battle, their ace Mega Evolves on its first turn out when it attacks.
  - In the Permanent modes, their ace is already Mega when the battle starts.

## With KIF: Elite Redux

- Its settings are in Options > KIF Settings > Mods > KIF: Elite Redux settings.
- **Without KIF: Elite Redux**, or with its Innates and Pokemon data settings both Off: official Megas, with their official ability, types and stats.
- **With it on** (Innates and Pokemon data are On by default):
  - Megas use their Elite Redux ability choice, 3 innates, and Elite Redux stats and types. For example, Mega Blastoise is Water/Steel.
  - 61 Elite Redux-only forms unlock.
- **Mixed settings:**
  - Innates Off with Pokemon data On gives official abilities but Elite Redux stats and types.
  - Turning off Pokemon data alone does not bring back official Megas.
- Elite Redux-only stones are only sold, and only work, while KIF: Elite Redux's Innates setting is On.

## Limits

- Cascoonite (Primal Cascoon) isn't sold yet: its ability isn't in the game.
- In a double battle, only one of your Pokémon can use the Mega button each turn: a Mega and a Manual Primal, or two Manual Primals, take two turns.
